Research trajectory

FROM ECUADOR TO BUDAPEST

I hold a BSc in Physics from ESPOCH and am pursuing an MSc in Astronomy at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE), Budapest. My work spans relativistic compact-object models, numerical solutions of Einstein equations and magnetic-field simulations.

My research interests include gravitational decoupling, realistic equations of state, relativistic stellar structure and gravitational-wave physics.
