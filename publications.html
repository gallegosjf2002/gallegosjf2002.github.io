Current research directions

CURRENT RESEARCH

Compact-star modeling with realistic nuclear EOS

Comparing tabulated dense-matter EOS and analytic representations, including piecewise polytropes, logarithmic-polynomial fits and shape-preserving interpolation. Investigating physically consistent TOV and exact-seed models.

CURRENT RESEARCH

Relativistic perturbations & gravitational-wave theory

Exploring feasibility of GD/MGDe extensions to time-dependent perturbations and rotating spacetimes; investigating stellar oscillations, tidal response and possible gravitational-wave observables.
